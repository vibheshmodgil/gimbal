Test one part at a time. Watch the real gimbal and the 3D twin: they should move the same way. Mark each step; your marks are kept in this browser.

1. Power & board -5 V 3 A adapter, common GND

Last reset -Up -Heap -PSRAM -Chip -Wi-Fi -Firmware -

BROWNOUT as the last reset = the 5 V supply dips when the servos move: use a 3 A adapter, the 470-1000 µF cap at the base, and thick 5 V/GND wires. Wi-Fi below -75 dBm makes the video stutter.

2. OLED screen -SDA 21, SCL 47, VCC 3V3

I2C bus sees -

Expect 0x3C. "none" = SDA/SCL swapped, loose, or no 3V3/GND. Re-plug the leads, then press Re-check wiring (no restart needed). All white with dead lines or blocks = a damaged panel.

3. Pan servo (MG996R) -signal GPIO 14, red 5 V adapter

Signal -Pulse - µsAngle -°

"→" should turn the camera to its right, the same as the twin. Wrong way: set PAN_INVERT 1. Twitches but won't hold: no common GND or a weak supply. Nothing at all: signal lead on the wrong pin. Signal off should make it go limp; if it still holds, the wire isn't on GPIO 14.

4. Tilt servo (MG90S) -signal GPIO 2, red 5 V harness

Signal -Pulse - µsAngle -°

"Up" should raise the camera, the same as the twin. Wrong way: set TILT_INVERT 1. If the head rubs the yoke at ±45°, adjust the trim on the Control tab.

5. Laser -NPN base via 1 kΩ on GPIO 1

Point it at a wall first. No light: check the transistor's C/B/E order (S8050 and 2N2222 pinouts differ) and that laser + goes to 5 V. Always on: collector and emitter are swapped. It switches itself off after 60 s.

6. Camera -built in (camera ribbon)

Sensor -Stream - fps

No sensor = the ribbon isn't fully latched. Picture upside down or mirrored: use Flip/Mirror on the Camera tab.

7. Status LED on-board RGB, GPIO 48

Normal colours: blue = ready, green = someone viewing, red = laser on, amber = no network.

The BOOT button on the board also changes pages (short press). A long press centres the gimbal and switches the laser off.
