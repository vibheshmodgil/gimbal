Setup · -

Once after building, or after taking the head off. Every button saves on the gimbal straight away.

1. Mount: same travel both ways

Pan
Tilt

Press Middle and wait until it stops. Undo the horn screw, pull the part off the servo spline and push it back on pointing straight ahead (tilt: level), screw it in. Or press an End and put the part back on turned fully to that side. Spline teeth are ~14° apart, so just get close: step 3 trims the rest.

2. Direction: match the buttons

Seen from behind: +pan must turn right, +tilt must look up. Wrong way: press Reverse (orange = reversed). The head doesn't move when you press it.

3. Zero: camera straight ahead and level

Pan
Tilt

4. Limits

Jog with step 3 to each end. Stop just before anything rubs or a cable pulls, then set that end.
